Off The Sofa

Coming soon to Google Play

A running app for getting off the sofa and building up from there. Follow a plan of laps by distance or time, see how fast you're going right now (not just your average), hear spoken cues as you run, and add heart rate from a Bluetooth sensor. Look back over your runs and see how you're improving.

Your runs stay on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking. Privacy policy
