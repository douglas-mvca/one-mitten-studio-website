Off The Sofa privacy policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Off The Sofa is a running app made by One Mitten Studio (Douglas Smith). This page explains what the app uses, where your information is kept, and what you can do about it.

  • Your runs stay on your phone. The app has no sign-in and sends nothing to us, so we never receive your data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared by us.
  • Your heart rate is health information. It never leaves your phone, except inside your own phone backup.
  • Google is involved in two places, both described below: an optional map behind a finished run, and your phone's own backup.

What the app uses, and why

  • Precise location, during a run only. From when you tap Start until you tap Finish, the app uses GPS to measure distance, pace and your route. This carries on with the screen off or the app in the background, which is why a "Running" notification shows the whole time. The app does not use your location at any other time.
  • Heart rate, if you connect a sensor. If you choose a Bluetooth heart rate sensor in Settings (such as a chest strap, an armband, or a watch set to broadcast its heart rate), the app reads your heart rate from it during a run and remembers the sensor so it can reconnect. Heart rate is health information, so we treat it as the most private thing the app holds: it is kept on your phone, it is never sent to us or anyone else, and it will never be part of any data we ask you to share. The "Nearby devices" permission is used only to find and connect to the sensor, not to work out where you are. Whatever device your heart rate comes from, now or in future versions, it is handled the same way.
  • Notifications show the run in progress and a Lap button.
  • Spoken messages and vibration (if switched on) use your phone's own text-to-speech and vibration.

What is kept on your phone

The app saves, in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your runs: date and time, distance, duration, laps, pace, heart rate, and the route you took;
  • your run plans;
  • your settings, including the heart rate sensor you chose and, if you give them, your age and max heart rate (used only to work out your heart rate zones).

Other apps cannot read this storage.

Google Maps (off unless you turn it on)

A finished run's route is drawn on your phone as a simple diagram. If you turn on Settings → Show a map under the route, the app also shows it on a Google map. To draw the map, your phone requests map images of that area from Google, so Google can see the general area of the run, along with the technical details any app shares when it loads a map (such as your IP address). This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Your heart rate and the rest of your run are not sent. Turn the setting off again at any time.

Backup to your Google account

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android includes Off The Sofa's data in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so your runs and plans come back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Google encrypts these backups, and we cannot see them. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings (usually Settings → System → Backup).

Deleting your data

  • Delete a run: open it in Past runs and tap Delete run.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • Copies in your Google backup are managed in your Google account and phone backup settings.

Not a medical device

Off The Sofa is for fitness and enjoyment. Heart rate, pace and the other figures it shows are estimates from your phone's GPS and your heart rate sensor, and can be wrong. The app does not diagnose, treat or monitor any medical condition and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor. If you have any concerns about your health or about exercising, talk to a doctor first, and stop if you feel unwell while running.

If this ever changes

Today the app sends nothing to us. In future we may add things that need our help: for example an account to keep your run plans and share them with friends, running groups and teams, or a way to share GPS recordings to help make distance and pace more accurate on different phones. Whatever we add will follow these rules:

  • Your choice. Accounts and sharing will be optional, off until you turn them on, and explained first. Everything the app does today will keep working without an account.
  • Only what you choose to share. You decide what goes to our servers and who sees it, such as a plan you share or a run you post to your group. Nothing else is sent.
  • Heart rate stays on your phone. It will never be sent to us, with or without an account.
  • GPS recordings for accuracy work will be made anonymous before they reach us (for example by leaving out the start and end of each run) and not linked to your name or account.
  • You can leave. Deleting your account will delete what you shared from our servers.
  • Never sold, never used for ads.
  • Described here first. This policy will be updated before any version of the app with these features is released.

Children

Off The Sofa is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
